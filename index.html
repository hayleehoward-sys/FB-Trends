03 · Recommendation

Ten trends to take into deep dive.

From the 42 trends measured, we recommend the following 10 for full deep-dive analysis. The selection balances four criteria (below) and ensures every benefit platform is represented.

This is a starting recommendation — the team can adjust the shortlist together before the final 10 is locked.

Selection Criteria
01

Volume

Does the trend have enough conversation to study meaningfully? We anchored on F&B-filtered global mentions.

02

Momentum

Is it growing or fading? We weighted YoY growth alongside absolute volume to surface where energy is moving.

03

Portfolio fit

Is there a credible Danone product or format hook? We mapped each trend to the existing portfolio (HiPRO, YoPRO, Activia, Actimel) and to white-space R&D opportunities.

04

Defensibility

Is the underlying evidence base credible? We dropped trends with safety warnings or pseudoscientific claims to protect brand integrity.

Platform Balance

Every benefit platform gets at least one pick; larger platforms get two. Health & Immunity and Longevity each receive one given their smaller F&B footprint.

The Top 10

Recommended for exclusion

These trends fail our defensibility filter or sit outside Danone's core portfolio. Each is flagged explicitly in our analyst notes.

  • Berberine. Liver-toxicity warnings issued by ANSES (FR), TGA (AU), AEMPS (ES). The "Nature's Ozempic" framing is misleading: different mechanism. Our analyst note recommends exclusion on safety grounds.
  • Japanese Mounjaro / Lemon Ginger Water. DIY GLP-1 mimic recipe with no scientific basis. Eli Lilly trademark takedown activity. Our analyst note recommends exclusion.
  • Oatzempic. ~85% search decline since peak. Mayo Clinic, BDA, DGEM, AEND, IstoÉ all issued debunks. Retained as a cautionary archetype only — not a deep dive.
  • Sober-Curious NoLo Beverages. Highest single-trend volume (263K mentions) but alcohol-replacement category sits outside Danone's dairy / water / yogurt portfolio. Worth monitoring as a behavioural signal, not deep-dive material.
